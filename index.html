ABOUT ME

The person
behind the work.

I’m Nusrat Afsana Usha, a UX/UI Designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

I’m interested in the space between people, information and technology — especially where thoughtful design can make something easier to understand or use.

My background in operations, administration and stakeholder communication has also shaped the way I approach design. I think about not only how something looks, but how it works within a real environment.

Today, my focus is on creating digital products, websites and interfaces that balance usability with a strong visual point of view.
