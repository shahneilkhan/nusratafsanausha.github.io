05 / ABOUT

Design
with context.

BASED IN Dhaka, Bangladesh
STUDYING MA UX Design London School of Design and Marketing
WORKING AT Website Deals UI Designer & Partner
PRIMARY TOOL Figma

I'm a UX/UI designer and Master's student in UX Design with a background that started outside the design industry.

Before design, I spent over six years working in operations and stakeholder management within the shipping industry. That experience taught me how to listen carefully, manage expectations and solve practical problems.

Today, I bring that same mindset into digital product design. I look at what the business needs, what users are trying to do, and how those two things can meet inside a simple interface.
