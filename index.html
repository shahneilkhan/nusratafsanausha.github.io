About Nusrat

Designing with curiosity, structure and intention.

I am Nusrat Afsana Usha, a UX/UI Designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I enjoy exploring how people interact with digital products and how thoughtful design can make those interactions clearer and more useful.

My background combines design, administration, communication and business education. This gives me a practical perspective when working through complex problems and collaborating with different stakeholders.

I am currently studying MA UX Design at the London School of Design and Marketing while continuing to develop my practice in UX/UI, research, prototyping and digital product design.
