I believe good design should feel effortless. Behind every intuitive interface is a thoughtful process: understanding people, simplifying complexity and making digital experiences feel natural.

I'm currently pursuing a Master's degree in UX Design at the London School of Design and Marketing. Before transitioning into design, I spent over six years working in operations, administration and stakeholder management in the shipping industry.

That experience shaped my approach to design. I listen carefully, understand business requirements and translate user needs into clear, functional interfaces.

At Website Deals, I design client websites, develop interface concepts and collaborate on marketplace operations and project delivery.
