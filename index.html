About me

Curious about people and systems.

I am Nusrat Afsana Usha, a UX/UI Designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My design practice is grounded in understanding people, organizing information and creating interfaces that feel clear and natural to use.

Alongside design, my professional experience in operations and administration has shaped how I approach communication, coordination, structure and real-world constraints.

I am currently pursuing an MA in UX Design at the London School of Design and Marketing, continuing to deepen my understanding of human-centered digital experiences.
