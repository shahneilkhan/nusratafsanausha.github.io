About me

A designer with a broader view.

I’m interested in the space where people, systems, and interfaces meet.

My background combines design, administration, operations, communication, and business education. That perspective helps me approach UX not only as visual design, but as a way of solving real problems with structure and intention.

I am currently pursuing an MA in UX Design at the London School of Design and Marketing, while continuing to develop my practice across digital products and interfaces.
