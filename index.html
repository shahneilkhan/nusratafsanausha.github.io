05 / ABOUT NUSRAT AFSANA USHA

Curious about people, systems and the small details that shape an experience.

My design journey is rooted in a combination of structured thinking, communication and curiosity about how people interact with digital products.

I enjoy working through ambiguous problems, organizing information and transforming complex requirements into experiences that feel approachable.

Alongside professional experience in operations and administration, I am continuing my academic journey in UX Design. This combination gives me a practical perspective on both people and processes.

I believe thoughtful design is often less about adding more and more about deciding what deserves attention.
