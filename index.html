04 / Design Principles

What guides the work

Design should remove friction.

01

Clarity

Make the important thing obvious before making the interface decorative.

02

Consistency

Build patterns that make interfaces predictable and easier to learn.

03

Purpose

Every visual decision should support a meaningful user or business goal.

04

Empathy

Design from the user's context rather than from assumptions about what they need.

05 / About
Nusrat Afsana Usha Dhaka / Bangladesh

A little about me

I care about the space between a user's intention and the interface.

I’m a UX/UI Designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, interested in how thoughtful structure, visual communication and human behavior come together inside digital products.

My approach combines research, information architecture, interaction design and visual systems to create experiences that are useful, understandable and considered.

Alongside design, my professional experience in operations and administration has shaped the way I think about communication, organization, stakeholders and real-world constraints.
