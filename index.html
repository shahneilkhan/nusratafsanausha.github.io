About Me

Designing with purpose.

Good design isn't only about how something looks. It's about how naturally people can understand, use and enjoy it.

Turning ideas into meaningful experiences.

I'm Nusrat Afsana, a UI/UX Designer passionate about creating digital products that feel clear, elegant and human.

I enjoy understanding the people behind every product, exploring their needs, identifying problems and transforming those insights into intuitive interfaces.

From early wireframes to polished prototypes, I focus on creating experiences that balance usability, aesthetics and business goals.
