About me

Design is about making things easier.

I am Nusrat Afsana Usha, a UX/UI Designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My work sits between user needs, business context and visual communication. I enjoy taking complex ideas and shaping them into experiences that feel clear, purposeful and considered.

My background in operations and administration has also shaped the way I approach design — with an emphasis on organization, communication, coordination and practical problem solving.
